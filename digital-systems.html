Worth a look

You may need more than a website if…

  • Your team enters the same information more than once.
  • Leads are followed up by hand, or not at all.
  • Customer information is scattered across different tools.
  • Your website does not talk to your internal systems.
  • The business runs on spreadsheets.
  • Too much of the week goes on repetitive admin.
  • Customers have to call or email for information they could look up themselves.
  • The systems that worked at your old size are getting hard to manage.

None of these necessarily mean you need more software. Often they just mean the systems you already have need to work better together.
