Privacy Policy

How we handle the personal data you give us through this website. Last updated: 18 August 2026.

Who we are

ErgonSite, Larnaca, Cyprus. For anything in this policy, contact us at info@ergonsite.com.

What we collect, and why

The only personal data this website collects is what you type into the contact form: your name, email address, company, message, and optionally a phone number. We use it for one purpose — to read and reply to your enquiry. We do not use it for marketing, we do not add you to any mailing list, and we do not sell or share it with anyone for their own purposes.

Who processes it on our behalf

When you press Send, your enquiry is delivered to our inbox by Web3Forms, and a confirmation email is sent to you through EmailJS. Both act only as delivery services. The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, which — like any web host — records standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to serve and protect the site.

Cookies and analytics

Essential storage is always active — it is limited to remembering the cookie choice you make and keeping the site working. Optional analytics stay off unless you allow them, either from the banner or from “Cookie settings” in the footer of every page.

NameWhat it doesLifetime
ergonsite-consent Essential. Remembers whether you allowed or declined analytics, so the banner does not reappear on every visit. Stored in your browser’s local storage. Until you clear it or change your choice
_ga, _ga_* Optional. Google Analytics measurement of page views and traffic sources. Set only after you allow analytics. Up to 2 years

In our Google consent settings, advertising storage, ad user data and ad personalisation are permanently denied — we measure the site, we do not do remarketing. The fonts the site uses are served from our own domain, so no request is made to third-party font servers when you browse.

How long we keep your data

Enquiry emails are kept in our inbox for as long as needed to handle your enquiry and any work that follows from it, and are reviewed and deleted when no longer needed, at most after 24 months.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us at any time what data we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, and object to our processing of it. Email info@ergonsite.com and we will respond within a month. You also have the right to complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection of Cyprus (dataprotection.gov.cy).
