Add ErgonSite to your home screen

One tap and it sits with your apps — opens full screen, no browser bars.

Installed. Look for the ErgonSite icon with your apps.

  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari — the square with an arrow pointing up.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top right.
  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top right of Chrome.
  2. Tap Add to Home screen, or Install app if you see it.
  3. Confirm with Install.
  1. Look for the install icon at the right-hand end of the address bar.
  2. Click it, then choose Install.
