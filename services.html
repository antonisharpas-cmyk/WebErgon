How we work

Five steps, and the fifth one never stops.

What actually happens, from the first call to the month after launch.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Before anything is designed we work out what the project actually is: who it is for, what it has to achieve, and which of the services above it needs. Scope is agreed in writing, so nothing is a surprise later.

    • Goals, audience and competitors
    • Scope and service mix agreed
    • Content and data audit
    • Written proposal
  2. 02

    Brand & Design

    Identity first, interface second. If the brand needs work we settle that first (logo, colours, type, guidelines) and only then design the pages, screens and flows that sit on top of it.

    • Brand identity and guidelines
    • Wireframes and page flow
    • Visual design, screen by screen
    • Review rounds until it is right
  3. 03

    Build

    The site, store, application or system gets built, connected to the tools you already use, and tested on real devices as it goes, not in one nervous pass at the end.

    • Front end and back end
    • Integrations and automations
    • Your content loaded in
    • Cross-device and browser testing
  4. 04

    Launch

    Migration, final checks and go-live. Analytics and search tools are connected before launch rather than after, so from day one you can see what people actually do.

    • Domain, hosting and SSL
    • Data and content migration
    • Analytics and Search Console
    • Handover and training
  5. 05

    Grow & Support

    Launch day starts this step, it does not finish the project. One side keeps everything running, the other keeps it growing, and you can take either on its own.

    • Hosting, backups and monitoring
    • Security patches and updates
    • SEO, content and campaigns
    • Technical support when you need it
